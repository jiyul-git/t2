## What a spot is
`p<stack>v1_<opener>_vs_sb_3b40`: the opener (BTN / HJ / LJ / UTG+2) opens 2.0, the SB 3-bets 4.0x, the opener answers (fold / call / 4-bet 2.15x-2.35x / jam; 5-bet jam). One HU subgame, the call terminals are corrected with 24-board flop panels (rule v4 loop), max 3 steps,
quick grade (converged earlier -> stop; else mean of the last 2 steps, `stopped_at_step_cap: true`), no P144. Specs already exist: `data/gto_spot/plan_a/specs/<id>.json` (generated by tools/gto_spot/plan_a_3bet_rest_specs.py). About 9 hours per spot on 4 cores.
